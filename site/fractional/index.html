Fractional Product Leadership

I partner with you as a fractional CPO or product leader, at the level your team needs, helping your team solve meaningful customer problems and achieve your business goals.

When fractional leadership helps

Sometimes you need experienced product leadership for a defined period, with fewer hours than a full-time role. Here are three ways I can help — contact me for more details.

Time to make the right hire

Finding the right person takes time, but product decisions and your team’s needs can’t wait. I step in to lead the work while you hire, then help onboard your new product leader, so they can get up to speed and take ownership.

A temporary leadership gap

When a product leader takes parental leave or another extended absence, I keep the team moving, support key decisions, and ensure a smooth handoff when they return.

A period of change

A new product, a shift in strategy, or a growing team can call for extra leadership capacity. I provide extra leadership to clarify priorities, guide the transition, and set your team up to carry the work forward.
